Deadlock Build Calculator

Senior Software Engineering Capstone · Team project · In progress

In Deadlock, players buy items to change how their hero performs. Comparing builds can be difficult because changing one item can affect several stats at once.

My team is building a calculator that lets players choose a hero, add or remove items, and see how those choices change the hero’s stats. For example, a player could swap a damage item for a defensive item and compare how the two builds affect damage and survivability before trying either build in the game.
